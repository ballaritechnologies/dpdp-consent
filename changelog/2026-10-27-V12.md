## [Unreleased] - 2026-10-27
### Summary
One consent record per visitor, moving with them between profiles, and SQLite search fixed. A visitor who decides again under another profile (e.g. moving between the DPDP and GDPR flows of `dpdp_gdpr`) now has their record moved to that profile. A visitor with several records from older data gets the newest one, on every driver.
### Added
- `UpdateConsentInput.profileId`: moves a consent record to another profile.
- Tests: `consent-profile-switch.integration.ts` (through the server), and `consent-storage.integration.ts` on every driver (profile move, legacy duplicate records, free-text search).
### Changed
- `getConsent(visitorId)` returns the visitor's most recently updated record on every driver, should there be more than one.
- `updateConsent` changes that record only, by its ID, instead of every record of the visitor.
### Fixed
- A visitor who decided again under another profile kept the old `profileId` on their record, with the new profile's version and consent. The record's signature, made over the new profile, then no longer matched, so verification reported `hmac_invalid`.
- SQLite (`node:sqlite`, `better-sqlite3`, `node-sqlite3-wasm`): searching the consents, visitors or audit-log lists (`q`) failed with an SQL syntax error. `ESCAPE` is now applied to each `LIKE`.
### Breaking changes
### Migration
None. Records already affected (old `profileId`, new consent) are fixed on the visitor's next decision.
