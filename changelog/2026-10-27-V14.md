## [Unreleased] - 2026-10-27
### Summary
Dashboard sessions follow the user's current state. In `local` auth mode, each admin request now checks the signed-in user against the database. Deactivating or deleting a user, or changing their roles or sites, takes effect at once instead of when the 30-minute session token expires.
### Added
- Test: a site granted to a user works with their open session, and a deactivated user is refused on their next request (`tenant-isolation.integration.ts`).
### Changed
- `authenticate()` in `local` mode loads the user, their roles and permissions, and their `allowedTenants` from storage on each request. `jwt` mode, whose tokens come from an outside issuer, still uses the token's claims.
### Fixed
- A deactivated or deleted dashboard user kept access through their open session for up to 30 minutes.
- A site added to a user's `allowedTenants` stayed out of reach until they signed in again; a site removed stayed reachable.
