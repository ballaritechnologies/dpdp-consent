## [Unreleased] - 2026-10-27
### Summary
Breach incident module (Phase 7): an incident log with a 72-hour countdown to the Data Protection Board report, reminders at 48/24/6 hours and when overdue, recorded milestones, and DRAFT notices for the Board and affected people rendered from editable template files. Ijazat never sends a notice.
### Added
- `BreachService` (incidents stored as `breach_incident` entities; validation; history; audit log), a 15-minute reminder job emitting the server event `breach:deadline`.
- Editable templates `apps/api/templates/breach/` (Board intimation, Board report, notice to people in English; notice to people in Hindi), copied to `dist/templates/breach/` on build; `compliance.breachTemplatesDir` to use your own. Every draft starts with `DRAFT - REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW`.
- Admin routes: `GET|POST /incidents`, `GET|PATCH /incidents/:id`, `POST /incidents/:id/milestones`, `POST /incidents/:id/notices`, `GET /export/incidents`; OpenAPI updated.
- Dashboard **Incidents** page: list with live countdown, log/edit form, milestones, notice drafts (copy/download), history. Strings in all six locales.
- Types: `BreachIncident`, `BreachIncidentFields`, `BreachDeadlineEvent` and related.
- Docs: `docs/breaches.md`, docs-site guide `/guides/backend/breaches`, event/route/config references, `apps/api/README.md`; legal questions Q-B1 to Q-B4.
- Tests: `breach.integration.ts`.
